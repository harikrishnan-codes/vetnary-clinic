Please call our 24/7 emergency hotline at +1 (800) 555-VETS immediately so our surgical trauma staff can prepare the emergency bay before your arrival. If possible, keep your pet calm, wrap them gently in a warm blanket, and avoid giving any human medications, food, or water before evaluation.

Our hospital is accredited in Fear-Free practices, which means our team is trained to identify and eliminate emotional stress triggers. We provide separate dog and cat examination wings, utilize species-specific pheromones (Adaptil & Feliway), use warm non-slip exam tables, and offer gentle handling paired with organic reward treats throughout the visit.

Standard procedures require withholding food starting at 10:00 PM the evening before surgery, while fresh water can usually remain accessible until early morning. Our surgical nurse will contact you the day prior to confirm pre-operative blood screening, drop-off times, and medication guidelines.

Yes! We work directly with all major pet insurance providers (such as Trupanion, Nationwide, and Healthy Paws) and can process direct claims on eligible policies. For unplanned procedures, we also offer zero-interest and extended financing options through CareCredit and Scratchpay.

We recommend full comprehensive physical examinations once a year for healthy adult dogs and cats (aged 1 to 7 years). For puppies, kittens, and senior pets (over 7 years) or those managing chronic conditions, bi-annual (every 6 months) checkups ensure timely preventive care and metabolic screening.
